PROJECT CASE STUDY · FULL-STACK E-COMMERCE

tlik Marketplace

A full-stack marketplace application with product discovery and core shopping workflows.

The problem

An online marketplace needs more than a product catalogue: users need to discover products, manage a cart and wishlist, authenticate and complete payment flows.

What I built

The project combines a React client with a Flask backend, MySQL persistence and Stripe payment integration. The application covers authentication, product management, cart, wishlist and checkout capabilities.

What I learned

E-commerce workflows require careful handling of identity, product state, cart consistency and payment boundaries. Payment-provider integration should be treated as a distinct responsibility, not just another CRUD endpoint.
