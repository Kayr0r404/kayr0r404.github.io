TheQuide Author Platform

A platform for authors to showcase their work, combining a React interface with Django content services and Firebase real-time features.

The problem

Writers need a place to present their work and manage content through an experience that connects a user-facing frontend with backend content functionality.

What I built

TheQuide combines a TypeScript/React frontend, Django backend, Firebase real-time capabilities and MySQL data storage.
