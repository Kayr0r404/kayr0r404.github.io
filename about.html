About Me

My studies in Applied Mathematics and Operations Research trained me to approach complex problems analytically: identify the important variables, understand constraints, reason about possible solutions and evaluate outcomes. That way of thinking influences how I approach software design, debugging, data modelling and system architecture.

★ My Software Engineering Experience ★

As a graduate in the Cassava AI team, I have contributed to internal product development and technical initiatives. I initially took ownership of developing an internal application that supports product-management workflows and coordination across AI initiatives. I also contributed to an internal showcase for solutions in the product pipeline and backend functionality for a golf-event client and guest experience. These projects have helped me understand the importance of translating stakeholder needs into useful software while working within real product constraints.
