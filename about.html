A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

About me

Where I’m coming from

I’m Karabo Mahlare, a software developer with a BSc in Mathematical Sciences from Stellenbosch University. I’m currently studying towards a Software Engineering Diploma at WeThinkCode_. I enjoy building software that solves real problems, understanding how systems fit together and improving the quality of the code I write.

My studies in Applied Mathematics and Operations Research trained me to approach complex problems analytically: identify the important variables, understand constraints, reason about possible solutions and evaluate outcomes. That way of thinking influences how I approach software design, debugging, data modelling and system architecture.

What I’ve been working on

As a graduate in the Cassava AI team, I have contributed to internal product development and technical initiatives. I initially took ownership of developing an internal application that supports product-management workflows and coordination across AI initiatives. I also contributed to an internal showcase for solutions in the product pipeline and backend functionality for a golf-event client and guest experience. Some of this work is internal, so I describe it at a high level.

At WeThinkCode_, I have supported fellow students as a peer tutor, helping a group work through the Robot World project. This included practical support around refactoring, clean-code practices, database-layer work, CI/CD and Docker. Earlier, through the Nedbank YES Programme, I supported strategy and research work involving spreadsheet data cleaning and analysis, presentations and reports.

What I enjoy building

I’m particularly interested in backend engineering: APIs, authentication, databases, service integration and systems that communicate reliably. I enjoy exploring event-driven architecture and asynchronous messaging, and I’m developing my data engineering skills through hands-on work with SQL, data pipelines and Redpanda using the Kafka API.

I’m also interested in applied AI and the engineering behind AI-enabled products, including retrieval-augmented generation, model integration and the infrastructure needed to build useful and dependable applications.

How I work

I value clear communication, collaboration, maintainability and taking responsibility for the work I contribute. I’m comfortable learning unfamiliar technologies and codebases, asking questions when requirements are unclear, using feedback to improve and working through problems methodically. I aim to understand not only how to make something work, but also why a particular solution makes sense.
