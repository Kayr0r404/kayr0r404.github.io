Why I built Plus27

I built Plus27 to create a digital home for local South African communities—a single, area-first platform where people can connect, trade, and find opportunities close to where they live. Instead of treating community life as separate activities spread across different apps and groups, Plus27 brings them together in one place.

★ The problem I’m trying to solve →

Local information and opportunities are often fragmented and hard to trust: neighborhood discussions happen in one place, buying and selling in another, odd jobs are shared informally, and rental listings are mixed in with irrelevant results from far away. This makes it difficult for residents to discover what’s relevant to their area and for local vendors and service providers to gain visibility.

Plus27 addresses this by giving each community its own hub for conversations, events, and groups, alongside a local marketplace, vendor profiles with reviews, an odd-jobs board, and rental listings—so people see what matters in their area by default, while still being able to explore other communities when needed.
